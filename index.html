    Gameplay Manual

    Goal

    Win by being the last active player remaining. A player is eliminated when they have no owned countries or no troops in owned countries. Antarctica troops do not keep a player active.

    Round Flow

    1. At the start of a new round, recruits are calculated automatically.
    2. If anyone receives recruits, players submit recruit plans independently.
    3. Recruits are placed on the board only after every active player with recruits has submitted.
    4. Turns then continue in the same alphabetical snake order. Round endings never reset the order.

    Placing Recruits

    Click one of your owned countries on the map or use the Place Recruits box. Add all recruits to your plan, then click Submit Recruit Plan. You can place recruits in Antarctica if you own a country eligible to transfer there.

    Your Turn

    Click one of your countries on the map to see available actions. You may claim unowned bordering countries, attack legal targets, and then make transfers after pressing Finished Attacking. Always leave at least 1 troop behind in an origin country.

    Visibility

    Your map shows your countries, bordering countries, network-connected countries, visible Antarctica access, and countries in the same regions. Same-region-only countries reveal only limited information. The log also shows only events you are allowed to know.

    Attacks

    Normal land attacks use up to 3 attack dice. Maritime attacks use up to 2. Regional limited attacks use 1. Ties go to the defender. If you conquer a country, you must move troops in while leaving 1 behind.

    Nuclear Rules

    Attacking, claiming, or conquering a nuclear country can trigger retaliation equal to that country's magnitude. If you claim or conquer a nuclear country and cannot lose the full retaliation amount in troops, the unpaid remainder becomes future region bonus withholding.

    Starting on a nuclear country also creates a region bonus recruit penalty equal to that starting country's magnitude.

    Antarctica

    Antarctica cannot be owned or attacked. Multiple players can have troops there. It is visible and usable only if you own an eligible Antarctica-connected country.

    Forfeit

    Forfeiting removes your troops outside Antarctica and makes your countries unowned. Your Antarctica troops remain there, but you are no longer active.
